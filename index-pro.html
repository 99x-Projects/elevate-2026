01What it is

A hackathon about doing our real work better.

Take a real problem that keeps coming back on your project, use AI to make it better, and show the difference with a number.

It doesn't have to be an "agent." Anything AI-powered counts, as long as it genuinely helps: a code-review helper, a Claude skill you build, an AI test suite, a bug watcher, or a support assistant. Pick what fits your project. And you stay in charge — a person always decides what ships.

02Why we're doing it

AI is the biggest shift in how we build software in years.

ELEVATE is our chance to get ahead of it — as engineers and as a company. Sharpen our skills, deliver more of what our customers care about, cut out the tedious repetitive work, and enjoy figuring it out together.

Better for the customer, better for the team. The teams that learn to work this way now will be the ones doing the most interesting work tomorrow.

03The four North Star pillars

Pick the North Star pillar you're improving.

Every team chooses one of these to improve. It's the one thing you'll be judged on — so it stays fair and focused.

Pillar 01

Higher Pace

Get things done and shipped faster — shorter waits, quicker releases.

Pillar 02

Better Quality

Fewer bugs and less redoing work. Catch problems earlier.

Pillar 03

Extended Value

Deliver more of what the customer actually cares about.

Pillar 04

A Team That Thrives

Less boring, repetitive work — a happier, more focused team.

Tip: pick one pillar to focus on, two at most. Genuinely improving one thing beats spreading yourself thin across all four.
04How it works

Four simple steps.

1

Find a real pain

Something on your project that keeps costing you time — the slow task everyone dreads.

2

Pick the pillar

Decide which of the four pillars fixing it improves.

3

Use AI to fix it

Build the AI improvement that fits — a person still approves what ships.

4

Measure it

A before number this week, an after number with AI next week. Bring both.

About the number: pick one thing you can measure in two weeks. No live customer by event day? A careful estimate is fine — just show your working. What doesn't land is "it made us faster" with nothing to back it up.
05Ideas to steal

Not sure where to start? Borrow one of these.

These are the kinds of AI improvements teams already use across a project's lifecycle. Grab the one that hurts your project most — or invent your own.

Planning

Tickets & backlog

  • Ticket triage — sort and route incoming work
  • Backlog clean-up — find duplicates and stale items
Building

Writing code

  • AI pair-coding — you steer, it does the typing
  • Ticket-to-PR helper — drafts a pull request for review
  • Auto documentation — keeps docs in step with the code
Testing

Proving it works

  • AI code review — a first pass before a human looks
  • AI test suite — writes and maintains the tests
Shipping & running

Keeping it healthy

  • Dependency upgrades — safe updates, on autopilot
  • Incident first-response — early diagnosis while you catch up
  • Bug watcher — spots issues before customers do
Support

Helping customers

  • Support assistant — answers the easy tickets, escalates the rest
Feedback

Learning from users

  • Feedback summariser — turns tickets, reviews and NPS into clear themes

08FAQ

Quick answers.

Do I have to build an "agent"?
No. Any AI improvement counts — a PR review helper, a Claude skill, an AI test suite, a bug watcher, and so on. Pick whatever genuinely helps your project.
Do we need a live customer to score well?
No. If you can't reach production in two weeks, a careful estimate is fine — just show the working behind it.
What do we bring on the night?
One improvement running on your real project, the goal you picked, and two numbers: a before and an after (or estimated) result.
We don't know where to start.
Walk your project stage by stage and ask what keeps costing time. The Ideas to steal section is a menu of common starting points.
What happens to the winning teams?
The top teams share what they built with everyone, and the strongest ideas can grow into things the team — and our customers — actually keep using.
